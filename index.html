Study Information and Consent

Study Overview

You are invited to participate in an academic research study examining how consumers respond to product-related livestreaming content.

Study Procedure

If you agree to participate, you will be randomly assigned to watch one pre-recorded, livestream-style product presentation. After watching the entire video, you will be directed to a questionnaire about your perceptions and evaluations.

Voluntary Participation and Risks

Your participation is voluntary, and you may withdraw at any time. The anticipated risks are minimal. You must complete the video to proceed to the questionnaire.

Data and Confidentiality

Your responses will be collected through Google Forms and used for academic research and statistical analysis. Your Prolific ID will be automatically prefilled into the questionnaire to match your survey responses with your Prolific submission.

Research data will be accessible only to authorized researchers. Individual responses and Prolific IDs will not be disclosed in published research results.

Research Team and Contact
